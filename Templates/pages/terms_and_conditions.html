Terms & Conditions

Islington Student Marketplace • Last updated: August 2026

1. Platform Purpose & Eligibility

Islington Marketplace is an exclusive student-driven platform designed to facilitate peer-to-peer textbook trading, computing gadget sales, academic discussion blogs, and campus commerce for students of Islington College, Kathmandu.

By registering an account or browsing listings, you agree to comply with these Terms and Conditions and all applicable college codes of conduct.

2. Student Accounts & Email Verification
  • Users must register with an authentic student email address and verify their account using a 6-digit Email OTP code.
  • You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
  • Impersonation of college staff, student leaders, or other students is strictly prohibited.
3. Product Listings & Moderation
  • All item listings are submitted for administrative review before going live.
  • Sellers must accurately describe item condition.
  • Prohibited items include: hazardous materials, non-academic illegal goods, and counterfeit electronics.
  • Listing prices must reflect fair student pricing in Nepalese Rupees (NPR).
4. 24-Hour Live Student Auctions
  • Sellers can initiate a 24-hour live auction on approved inventory items with a minimum starting price.
  • All bids placed by authenticated students are binding.
  • The highest bidder at the expiration of the 24-hour window (or upon early seller acceptance) wins the item.
5. Safe Campus Meetups & Pickup Blocks

6. Payments & Settlement
  • Cash on Campus Pickup (COD): Buyers pay sellers directly upon physical inspection.
  • Digital Wallets (eSewa / Khalti Sandbox): Provided for online order settlement.
  • Islington Marketplace operates with 0% seller commission fees.
7. Blog & Community Forum Code of Conduct
  • Treat fellow students and moderators with respect and professional courtesy.
  • Do not post defamatory content, hate speech, or harassment.
  • Discussion of modules and coursework concepts is encouraged.
