Privacy Policy

Islington Student Marketplace • How we protect your data • Last updated: August 2026

1. Commitment to Student Privacy

At Islington Marketplace, your privacy and digital security are our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used to facilitate campus commerce and student blogs, and how we protect your personal records.

2. Information We Collect
  • Account Information: Student name, username, email address, and encrypted password credentials.
  • Verification Data: 6-digit email OTP codes used during registration and password resets.
  • Marketplace Activities: Product listings, photos, prices, descriptions, 24h auction bids, and shopping bag orders.
  • Campus Blog Posts & Comments: Academic questions, discussion threads, and peer replies posted on the platform.
3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for campus-related activities:

  • To authenticate verified Islington College students.
  • To connect buyers and sellers for physical handovers at designated campus blocks (e.g. Kumari Hall, Skill Block, Brit House).
  • To deliver automated email notifications for listing approvals, auction updates, outbid alerts, and order confirmations.
  • To maintain a respectful, spam-free student discussion environment.
4. Data Protection & Security
  • End-to-End Encryption: All web traffic is protected with modern HTTPS/SSL 256-bit encryption.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using industry-standard PBKDF2 algorithms.
  • Secure Media Storage: Item photos and blog attachments are stored on secure cloud storage with strict access controls.
5. Zero Third-Party Advertising

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Your information is never shared with third-party commercial marketing or advertising networks. Transaction details are only visible to the respective student buyer and seller involved in a campus trade.

6. Your Rights & Account Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can view and edit your profile details at any time in your Student Dashboard.
  • You can remove your active product listings or delete published blogs whenever you wish.
  • To request complete account deletion, contact our student support team.
Encrypted & Protected

Your credentials and transactions are secured with SSL encryption and Google SMTP OTP verification.
